About Sales Coach Picks

Sales Coach Picks was founded in 2019 by Renata Hess, a former enterprise sales coach who spent fifteen years working with teams ranging from scrappy three-person startups to regional divisions of large insurance companies. After watching countless coaching clients waste time and budget on tools that looked great in demos but fell apart in practice, Renata decided to build the resource she always wished had existed: an honest, independent guide to what actually helps salespeople sell.

The site launched with a simple promise to never accept payment for favorable reviews and to test every tool and read every book before saying a word about it. That commitment turned out to resonate. Within the first year, the newsletter crossed a thousand subscribers almost entirely through word of mouth among coaching communities and sales leadership forums. Renata brought on two contributing editors in 2021, both experienced coaches themselves, to keep up with the volume of tools flooding the market.

Today Sales Coach Picks is a small but trusted voice in the sales enablement world. The team stays lean by design. Every review is written by someone who has actually coached a sales team, which means the perspective is always practical and never purely academic. The goal has not changed since day one: help coaches and their teams make smarter decisions so they can spend less time evaluating and more time selling.

What we value

  • Independence: we do not accept payment, free licenses, or affiliate arrangements in exchange for favorable reviews
  • Practicality: every recommendation is evaluated through the lens of real coaching and real selling situations
  • Honesty: if something does not work well, we say so clearly and explain exactly why
  • Respect for time: coaches and sales leaders are busy, so we write to be useful fast
  • Continuous updating: markets and tools change, and our reviews change with them
