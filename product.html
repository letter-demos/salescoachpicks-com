Tool Reviews

In-depth, hands-on reviews of CRM platforms, prospecting tools, dialers, and sales enablement software rated by real coaches.

  • Scored on ease of use, ROI, and coaching applicability
  • Side-by-side comparisons for top competing tools
  • Updated quarterly to reflect new features and pricing changes
  • Includes a Coach Verdict summary at the end of every review

Book Picks

Curated reading lists and detailed reviews of sales books, organized by skill level and specialty area.

  • Categories include prospecting, negotiation, mindset, and leadership
  • Each book rated for actionability and relevance to coaching
  • Monthly featured pick with a full chapter-by-chapter breakdown
  • Reading roadmaps for new reps and experienced coaches alike

Training Program Guide

An annual guide to the best online courses, certifications, and live workshops available for sales professionals.

  • Programs vetted for curriculum quality and instructor credentials
  • Cost-per-outcome analysis for each program reviewed
  • Sorted by format: self-paced, cohort-based, and in-person
  • Special section dedicated to programs built for sales managers and coaches
